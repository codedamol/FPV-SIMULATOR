FPV Flight Simulator

Fixed-wing countryside flight with a landing field. On a tablet, hold it like a steering yoke: tilt left and right to roll, tilt forward and back to pitch, and drag the left slider for throttle.

Keyboard: arrows = roll and pitch (up arrow = nose down), A/D = yaw, W/S = throttle, C = camera, R = reset.
